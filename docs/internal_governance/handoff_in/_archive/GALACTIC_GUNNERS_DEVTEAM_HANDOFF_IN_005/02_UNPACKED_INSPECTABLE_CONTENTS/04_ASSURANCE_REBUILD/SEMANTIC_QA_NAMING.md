Create permanent semantic QA commands, not another handoff-number verifier as the long-term authority.

Required root scripts:

```text

```

Handoff 005 evidence may invoke those commands and archive their output under the handoff evidence folder.

Do not make:

```text

```

the permanent product test authority.

Existing handoff-specific scripts may remain historical until later cleanup.
