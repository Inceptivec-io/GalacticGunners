## 7. Royalty Calculation — Founder Direction v1.1

The commercial understanding is:

# **Aurora receives 40% of Galactic Gunners net profit/royalty base after transparent operating costs attributable to the product.**

The intent is that genuine costs of operating and selling Galactic Gunners are recovered first, while Aurora receives a clear and auditable 40% share of the remaining product return.

### Operating-cost principle

Permitted costs must be:

- genuinely attributable to Galactic Gunners;
- transparent;
- evidenced;
- consistently applied;
- commercially reasonable;
- not used to obscure or artificially reduce the royalty.

Expected cost categories are likely to be relatively fixed or directly customer/sales-volume driven.

### Included operating costs

Examples include:

- VAT/sales tax borne/remitted in relation to sales;
- Apple/Google/Steam/Microsoft/other storefront commission;
- payment processor fees;
- refunds and chargebacks;
- product-specific hosting/backend/database costs;
- product-specific domains/services;
- product-specific store/developer-account costs allocated on a reasonable basis;
- product-specific compliance/rating fees;
- product-specific third-party service fees;
- directly attributable paid marketing spend, where Founder-authorised;
- directly attributable commercial asset-production expenditure where external cost is actually incurred;
- other necessary Galactic Gunners operating costs that are documented and visible to both parties.

### Excluded / controlled costs

The business must not arbitrarily load unrelated Secuvara, Arqora or Inceptivec overhead into Galactic Gunners.

General group expenditure is not deductible unless:

1. it is genuinely attributable to operating Galactic Gunners; and
2. the allocation basis is reasonable and transparent.

Michael's or Aurora's ordinary contributed time is not automatically converted into an internal cost deduction unless separately agreed.

### Royalty formula

```text
Galactic Gunners Gross Receipts
− sales taxes / VAT where applicable
− storefront / platform commissions
− transaction fees
− refunds / chargebacks
− transparent Galactic Gunners operating costs
= Galactic Gunners Net Royalty Base

Aurora Royalty = 40% × Galactic Gunners Net Royalty Base
```

### Transparency rule

A royalty statement must make visible:

- gross receipts;
- each cost category;
- cost amount;
- basis of allocation where shared;
- resulting net royalty base;
- Aurora's 40% calculation.

The intent is a real profit-sharing opportunity with honest cost recovery, not a nominal royalty that can be reduced through opaque internal accounting.
