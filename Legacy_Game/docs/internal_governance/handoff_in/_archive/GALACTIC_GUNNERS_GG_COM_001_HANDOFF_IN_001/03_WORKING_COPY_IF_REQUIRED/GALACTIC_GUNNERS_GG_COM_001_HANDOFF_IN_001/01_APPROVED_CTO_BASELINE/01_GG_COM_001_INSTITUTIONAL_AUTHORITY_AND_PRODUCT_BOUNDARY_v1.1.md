---

## 2. CLOM Relationship

The correct rule is:

> **Authority derives through the CLOM-governed institutional hierarchy; Galactic Gunners execution remains external to the CLOM.**

Therefore:

- the CLOM is not cloned or copied into the product;
- the CLOM is not used as the Galactic Gunners working repository;
- Galactic Gunners code is not required to live in the CLOM;
- Development may execute through the product's own external boundary;
- the product remains subject to inherited Secuvara operating standards where applicable;
- the product cannot infer broader authority from technical access.

## 5. Inceptivec Gamification Boundary

Inceptivec Gamification is deliberately bounded from the serious GRC/governance estate.

Default rule:

```text
SECUVARA / GRC / GOVERNANCE PRODUCTS
→ NO DEFAULT GAMIFICATION

INCEPTIVEC GAMIFICATION
→ EXPLICIT INTERACTIVE / GAME / ENGAGEMENT DOMAIN
```

Professional simulation or training may use game mechanics where justified, but there is no authority to inject badges, scores, rewards or engagement gimmicks into institutional governance products merely because a gamification capability exists.

---

## 6. Commercial Capability Purpose

Inceptivec Gamification may support:

- owned commercial games;
- commissioned games;
- branded interactive experiences;
- simulation;
- professional training experiences;
- education;
- competition systems;
- scoring and ranking;
- achievements;
- leaderboards;
- engagement systems;
- reusable gamification technology;
- client delivery where similar products are requested.

Galactic Gunners is the first reference implementation.

---

## 7. Commercialisation Principle

The game is both:

1. a direct revenue product;
2. a capability demonstrator for future Inceptivec client work.

This must not cause over-engineering.

Reusable gamification components should be extracted when real product need proves them reusable, not pre-built as an unnecessary platform.
