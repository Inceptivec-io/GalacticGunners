## Superseded planning rule

Any predecessor/superseded Roadmap or Playlist currently present in the living planning root must be:

1. inventoried;
2. identified by version/currentness;
3. preserved unchanged;
4. moved to:

```text
docs/internal_governance/planning/_archive/
```

5. recorded as:

```text
SUPERSEDED / PRESERVED
```

6. removed from the living current planning root.

Do not delete predecessor planning.

Do not overwrite predecessor files.

Do not leave multiple Roadmaps/Playlists appearing CURRENT.

SUPERSEDED ROADMAPS = PRESERVED IN planning/_archive
