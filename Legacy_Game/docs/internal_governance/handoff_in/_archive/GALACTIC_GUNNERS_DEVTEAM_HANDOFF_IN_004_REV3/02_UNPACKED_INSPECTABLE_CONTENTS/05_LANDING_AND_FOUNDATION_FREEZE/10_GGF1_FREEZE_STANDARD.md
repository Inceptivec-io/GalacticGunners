# GGF-1 FREEZE STANDARD

If REV3 passes Founder review, the current legacy runtime becomes the **GGF-1 accepted behavioural baseline**.

This does not mean every future aesthetic refinement is complete.

It means the game is sufficiently:
- playable;
- collision-reliable;
- correctly scored;
- controllable;
- visually coherent;
- audio-correct;
- result-state correct;

to reproduce in GGF-2 production architecture.

Non-blocking refinements may be registered and carried into migration.

Critical gameplay defects may not.
