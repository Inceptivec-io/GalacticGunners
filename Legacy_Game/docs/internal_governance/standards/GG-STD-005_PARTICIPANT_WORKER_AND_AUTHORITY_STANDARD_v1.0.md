# GG-STD-005 — PARTICIPANT, WORKER AND AUTHORITY STANDARD v1.0

Identity, role and authority are distinct.

## Founder

Michael Leese:
- Founder/Product Authority;
- acceptance/merge authority.

## Aurora Leonardi

Human collaborating contributor.
Contribution and royalty participation do not independently grant repository/institutional authority.

## Worker

`_WORK_00000001_POST_BOX` is an exchange identity, not automatic proof of an admitted Worker.

Any Development Agent must be recorded truthfully in the Worker/Executor Register with:
- identity;
- assignment;
- Handoff;
- repo;
- branch;
- scope;
- status.

Capability does not confer authority.
