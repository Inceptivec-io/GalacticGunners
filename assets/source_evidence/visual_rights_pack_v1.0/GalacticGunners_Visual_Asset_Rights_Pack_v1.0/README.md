# Galactic Gunners Visual Asset Rights Pack v1.0

This pack provides editable ownership, licensing, contributor-assignment and filename-governance documents for the Galactic Gunners visual assets produced or referenced in the 24 August 2026 production session.

## Before relying on the pack

1. Replace every square-bracket placeholder, especially the final legal rights-holder name.
2. Confirm ownership or permission for user-supplied background inputs.
3. Obtain contributor signatures where another person supplied creative material.
4. Obtain qualified Italian/EU IP advice before investment, enforcement, assignment or material third-party licensing.

The documents deliberately distinguish contractual ownership of AI output from statutory copyright protection. They are evidence and contract templates, not a copyright registration or legal opinion.
